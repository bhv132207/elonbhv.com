The Odyssey

Elon Reeve Musk (born June 28, 1971) is a business magnate and investor. He is the founder, CEO, and Chief Engineer at SpaceX; angel investor, CEO, and Product Architect of Tesla, Inc.; founder of The Boring Company; and co-founder of Neuralink and OpenAI. With an estimated net worth of around $240 billion, implies he is the wealthiest person in the world.

1971

Origins: Born in Pretoria, South Africa. Displayed an early aptitude for computers and entrepreneurship.

1995

Zip2: Co-founded his first company, providing city guide software to newspapers. Sold to Compaq for $307 million.

1999

X.com & PayPal: Founded X.com, an online bank, which merged with Confinity to become PayPal. eBay acquired it for $1.5 billion.

2002

SpaceX: Founded to reduce space transportation costs and enable the colonization of Mars.

2004

Tesla: Joined as chairman and product architect to accelerate the advent of sustainable transport.

2016

Neuralink & The Boring Co.: Launched ventures to merge human brains with AI and solve traffic congestion.

2022

Twitter / X: Acquired the social media platform to protect free speech and create the "Everything App".
